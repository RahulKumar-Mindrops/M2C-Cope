Our Story

A Decade of Redefining Corporate Talent

MilitaryToCorp was established by military veterans who recognized a profound paradox: corporate India faced a severe crisis in reliable, ethical, crisis-resilient operational leadership, while thousands of highly trained officers were retiring annually in their prime.

The barrier was not competence; it was translation. Corporate HR teams lacked familiarity with military ranks and technical responsibilities, while veterans needed guidance in articulating their profound leadership experience in commercial terminology.

Over the past twelve years, M2C has systematically broken down these silos, becoming the premier partner for corporations seeking operational excellence and veterans seeking purposeful second careers.
