Important Information

Please read the following information carefully to stay informed and avoid any fraudulent activities.

BEWARE OF FRAUD Stay alert. Verify information only through official channels.
1

Do not believe misleading information or unauthorized job circulars being circulated on social media. For authentic, verified updates, visit the official MilitaryToCorp website regularly. Some unscrupulous persons/agencies have been making fake job offers using the Company's name, logo, and address. BAS/M2C has not authorized any agency or individual to recruit on its behalf or collect any money/commission/charges.

3

M2C never makes phone calls/SMS asking candidates to deposit money. Selected candidates receive communication strictly via official corporate channels. Keep your registration details and mobile numbers confidential.
