Clarity & Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do veterans need to pay any fee for M2C placement services?

No. MilitaryToCorp provides career counseling, resume evaluation, job application access, and interview scheduling to Indian military veterans 100% free of charge. We are compensated exclusively by client employers.

How does M2C verify military service credentials?

Our team includes veteran officers who review official discharge books, release orders, identity cards, and gallantry/citation records. We uphold military-grade verification standards before introducing any candidate to corporate clients.

What industries hire veterans through MilitaryToCorp?

Our partner network spans IT & Cloud technology, E-Commerce and 3PL logistics, manufacturing & heavy engineering, infrastructure, automotive, banking, financial services & insurance (BFSI), and pharmaceutical sectors.
